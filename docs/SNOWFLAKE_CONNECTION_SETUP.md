# Airflow Snowflake Connection

Create this in Airflow UI under Connections.

Connection ID:
`snowflake_weather`

Recommended fields:
- Account: your Snowflake account identifier
- Login/User: your Snowflake user
- Password or supported authentication method
- Warehouse: your warehouse
- Database: `WEATHER_ANALYTICS`
- Schema: `RAW`
- Role: your usable role

Do not take or submit a screenshot that exposes the actual password, token, or private key.
